# QuickBite Definition of Done

A Product Backlog Item (PBI) is considered **Done** when:

1. Its agreed acceptance criteria are satisfied.
2. Its changes are committed to the shared repository and reviewed by another team member.
3. Relevant unit and integration tests pass, including applicable invalid-input cases.
4. Its changes are integrated into the shared application.
5. Its functionality works with the other completed features.
6. No known defect prevents its intended use.
7. Necessary setup or usage documentation is updated.
8. The project-board item records completion and links to relevant review or testing evidence.

Coding completion alone does not mean Done. If required review, testing, integration, or documentation remains unfinished, the item is not counted as completed work in the Increment.
